Questions? Answers.

Do I need to check in at the same time every day?

No. Any check-in before midnight keeps your streak alive.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your streak resets unless you use a streak freeze. Momentum includes a few each month.

Can I share habits with friends?

Yes. Shared challenges let you build a streak together.

Is my data private?

Everything you track is visible only to you unless you choose to share it.
